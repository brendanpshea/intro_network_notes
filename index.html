Introduction to Networking

A free, openly licensed textbook covering networking fundamentals from the OSI model through security hardening — aligned to CompTIA Network+ exam objectives.

Format

Interactive HTML chapters with diagrams, plus downloadable PDF lecture slides for every module.

Scope

11 modules from physical cabling through security hardening — covering all Network+ domain areas.

Audience

Students new to IT networking; also useful as a refresher for those preparing for the N10-009 exam.

Chapters

OSI and TCP/IP models, network topologies (star, mesh, bus, ring), SOHO networks, troubleshooting methodology.
